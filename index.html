How it works

  1. 1

    Upload

    Add photos and videos from your phone or computer. Big videos upload in parts and can resume if the connection drops.

  2. 2

    Organise

    Sort everything into nested folders and choose the cover that represents the event.

  3. 3

    Publish

    Publish now, or schedule when the event goes live and when it stops being shared.

  4. 4

    Share one link

    Send the private link in any chat. It previews with the cover and the two latest photos.
